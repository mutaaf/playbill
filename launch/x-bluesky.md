**1/**
I made a football history game. Spin a franchise and an era, take one real player season, and fill seven slots. Your roster gets a rating, and the rating is a 17-game record.

The same roster always gets the same record. No random losses.

[link]

**2/**
4,872 rated seasons from 1980 to 2025. Each one is scored against its own era, so a 1999 receiver and a 2024 receiver can sit on the same roster fairly.

**3/**
17-0 is gated. Every slot needs to be All-Pro caliber, not just the total. Measured over 400,000 games, a perfect season lands about once in 6,000. 16-1 about once in 49.

**4/**
The default mode shows the stat line but hides the rating, so it's a football judgment, not a memory test. If a rating looks wrong, the game asks you after the season.

**5/**
Ranked seasons are dealt and scored by the server, so a modified client can't post a score it didn't earn. Free, no account, plays offline once installed.

COLD GAS: drive a roadster up a ramp, fire ten cold-gas thrusters over a row of parked cars, roll it 360 in the air, and land on the pad. Free in the browser, no account.

[link]

**2/**
The physics is a deterministic simulation at 240 Hz. Same inputs, same jump, on every machine.

**3/**
That's what keeps the leaderboard honest. A run is a log of inputs, and the server replays the log and scores it again before the run counts.

**4/**
Three judges score each jump. The numbers come from the scorer. The AI only writes the commentary, and you can bring your own key if you want different judges.

**5/**
